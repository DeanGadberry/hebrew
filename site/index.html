      Getting them onto your iPhone
      1. Download episodes here (on a Mac, Chrome or Edge lets you pick a folder and saves straight into it).
      2. Open the TV app on the Mac → File → Import… and pick the files. They appear under TV Shows → Aleph with Beth, in order.
      3. Plug in the iPhone, open it in Finder → TV Shows tab → tick Aleph with Beth → Sync.
      4. On the iPhone, open the TV app → Library. It remembers where you stopped and marks watched episodes.

      Audio-only files go to the Music app instead (File → Import). To keep your place, select them in Music → Get Info → Options → Remember playback position.

      Infuse, VLC and other players read the same episode tags.

      Settings

      The relay is a small Cloudflare Worker that passes the files through (Lets.Church doesn’t let other websites read them directly). See the project README.
